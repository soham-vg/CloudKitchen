About Us

Food Passion Since 2020

Hello and welcome to Aadya’s Non Veg Kitchen.

I am Radhika Sagar Gangavane, and cooking has always been one of the biggest passions of my life. For me, food is not just about recipes or ingredients. It is about emotions, memories, and bringing people together with great taste.

Since the beginning, I have loved preparing dishes for family, friends, and guests at home. Whether it was flavorful biryani, delicious non veg dishes, spicy pav bhaji, or homemade comfort meals, people always appreciated my cooking and encouraged me to do something bigger with this passion.

Their love, compliments, and support gave me the confidence to start this cloud kitchen journey in 2020. With strong encouragement from my family, I decided to turn my passion into something that could reach more people and make them happy through food.

Every dish prepared in my kitchen is made with fresh ingredients, proper hygiene, authentic flavors, and most importantly, genuine care. I always focus on serving food that feels homemade, satisfying, and memorable.

Cooking for people and seeing them enjoy the food gives me immense happiness and motivation every single day. My goal has always been simple — to serve delicious food that makes people feel connected, comfortable, and excited for their next meal.

Last but not least, I would like to thank my lovable daughter Aadya, who became the inspiration behind starting Aadya’s Non Veg Kitchen and this beautiful journey. Her name will always remain the heart of this dream.

Thank you for being part of this journey and supporting my dream. I truly hope every order from our kitchen brings taste, happiness, and beautiful moments to your table.
